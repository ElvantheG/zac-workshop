# Workshop Slides Template — 45-Slide Narrative Arc

> **Source:** Reverse-engineered from https://anti-prompt-slides.vercel.app/workshop (Zac Hansen's Anti-Prompt Method workshop)
> **Use case:** Build your own 60–90 minute workshop that ends with a paid upsell.
> **Total runtime:** ~85 minutes across 45 slides
> **Conversion mechanic:** Free education → case study proof → soft challenge → hard ask

---

## The 5-Phase Narrative Arc

| Phase | Slides | Duration | Role |
|-------|--------|----------|------|
| 1. Setup & Authority | 1–7 | 10 min | Build credibility before teaching |
| 2. Technical Setup / Frame | 8–16 | 15 min | Lower barrier to entry, install mindset |
| 3. Build & Demo | 17–28 | 30 min | Core teaching + live proof |
| 4. Selling Strategy | 29–39 | 20 min | Position offer correctly |
| 5. Close & Upsell | 40–44 | 10 min | CTA + paid pitch |

**Total: 85 min · 45 slides**

## Slide-by-Slide Template

### **Phase 1: Setup & Authority (Slides 1–7) — 10 minutes**

| # | Slide Title | What Goes Here | Purpose |
|---|-------------|----------------|---------|
| 1 | [Method Name] | Headline promise: "Create [Outcome] That Get [Audience] Day After Day" | Hook + promise |
| 2 | How to Get the Most Out | Rules: participate, ask questions, execute | Tone setting + interactive contract |
| 3 | 30 Second Pitch | Mention paid offer ("Five spots... [Sprint name]") | **Plant seed for upsell** (gentle, no hard sell yet) |
| 4 | Install [Method] | Core promise restated + "while saving X hours" | Reinforce value prop |
| 5 | How We're Going to Get There | 3 parts: Setup, Build, Sell | Roadmap clarity, removes mystery |
| 6 | Who Am I to Help You | Credentials: revenue numbers, client wins | Build authority |
| 7 | Why [Method] is the Future | Case study: $X launch → $Y month | **Social proof — first callback to use later** |

**Key tactic:** Authority (slide 6) BEFORE case study (slide 7). Credibility precedes proof.

| 9 | [Tool] Has 3 Modes | Orient audience to the tool you'll use today | Technical orientation |

**Key tactic:** Each slide answers a silent objection. Slide 10 removes biggest fear ("I'm not technical"). Slide 13 removes overwhelm. Slide 15 is where the architecture lands.

| 24 | Brainstorm Your Tool First | Process: AI interviews you before building | Prevents wrong-thing-built |
| 25 | The 3 System Prompts | Copy-paste templates (the actual deliverables) | **The deliverable — exact tools** |

**Key tactics:**

- Slide 25 is where you GIVE the actual templates (the audience came for this)
- Slide 27 is the live proof that everything you taught works

### **Phase 4: Selling Strategy (Slides 29–39) — 20 minutes**

| # | Slide Title | What Goes Here | Purpose |
|---|-------------|----------------|---------|
| 29 | Selling Section Header | "Your [core service] is product. Tools make irresistible." | **Narrative pivot to monetization** |
| 30 | The Beta Case Study Play | "Members know it's beta, bugs reported, fixed live" | Normalizes iteration, removes perfection pressure |
| 31 | The Biggest Mistake | "I'll sell this as [wrong-positioning]" — crossed out | **Anti-pattern warning** |
| 32 | What IS the Main Thing? | Hierarchy: core service = sun, tools = orbiting planets | **Positioning realignment** |
| 33 | The Offer Stack | Core Offer + 3 bonus layers | Shows package architecture |
| 34 | The 90-Day + MRR Mechanic | Pricing model timeline | **Monetization mechanism explained** |
| 35 | What Keeps It Running? | Hosting cost, maintenance time, fix frequency | Removes "this will collapse" fear |
| 36 | Why 90 Days Works | Long enough for results, short enough for urgency | Justifies window choice |
| 37 | Real Numbers: Pricing Breakdown | $X offer × Y units = $Z launch | **Callback to slide 7 — full math now** |
| 38 | Positioning Language That Works | 4 copy swipes (exact phrases) | Gives words to use |
| 39 | The Positioning Checklist | 7-item gate before going live | Quality control |

**Key tactics:**
- Slide 31 prevents the audience from shipping wrong offer
- Slide 32 reframes — your CORE thing stays the product, tools are bonuses
- Slide 37 is the callback to Slide 7 — moves audience from "is this possible?" to "here's exactly how"
- Slide 38 gives them the words so they don't have to invent them

### **Phase 5: Close & Upsell (Slides 40–44) — 10 minutes**

| # | Slide Title | What Goes Here | Purpose |
|---|-------------|----------------|---------|
| 40 | You Have Method/Tools/Positioning | Recap what they got | Tone shift to close |
| 41 | Your 72-Hour Challenge | Pick one tool, load content, ship, share | **First CTA: post-workshop execution** |
| 42 | The [Sprint Name] | "Five spots... flip knowledge into $X/month... 1 hour/day" | **Paid pitch begins (soft)** |
| 43 | Next Step Offer Details | $X workshop credit + $Y match = $Z entry | **Hard ask — friction removed** |
| 44 | Questions? Drop in Chat | Resources page + Sprint doc links | Open close, stay available |

**Key tactic — the soft-to-hard pivot:**
- Slide 40 = "you have everything"
- Slide 41 = "go ship something" (free CTA)
- Slide 42 = soft mention of paid offer
- Slide 43 = specific price with friction-removal incentive
- Total time from "free recap" to "hard ask": **1–2 minutes**

## Master Patterns (Use Across All Slides)

### 1. The Callback Pattern
- **Plant** a key piece of proof early (Slide 7)
- **Reference** it during teaching ("remember that case study?")
- **Fully unpack** at sales section (Slide 37)
- Effect: moves audience from "is this possible?" to "here's exactly how"

### 2. Objection Removal Arc
Each phase should have at least one slide that removes a silent objection:
- Phase 2: "I'm not technical" → Slide 10
- Phase 2: "I'm overwhelmed" → Slide 13
- Phase 3: "I don't have content" → Slide 26
- Phase 4: "It will be too expensive to maintain" → Slide 35
- Phase 4: "I'll just position it as SaaS" → Slide 31

### 3. Permission-Giving Reframes
- "We live in [build mode] today" (permission to build)
- "You don't need to code. You need to rant." (permission to use natural language)
- "Brainstorm First" (permission to NOT execute immediately)

### 4. Deliverable-Then-Validation
- First **drop the actual templates** (Slide 25)
- Then **show them working** (Slide 27)
- Effect: utility precedes proof, not the other way around

### 5. The Anti-Pattern Pivot
Always include one "biggest mistake" slide before pitching paid offer.
- Names what NOT to do
- Reframes what TO do
- Prevents wrong-offer-shipped after workshop

- Phase 1: 7 min · 5 slides

- Phase 3: 22 min · 9 slides
- Phase 4: 14 min · 7 slides
