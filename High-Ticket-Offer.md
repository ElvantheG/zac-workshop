# High-Ticket Offer Definition

## The One-Line Offer

**Help former personal trainers add $10K/month in 10 hours/week — using their existing expertise, their Human Design, and AI tools to sell.**

---

## Why This Audience

Former personal trainers:
- Already have coaching expertise (they just hate trading hours for money)
- Understand the body-energy connection (HD is not a stretch for them)
- Want freedom, not another job
- You already have a list of them

You don't need to teach them how to be experts. You need to show them how to package and sell what they already know — aligned to how they're naturally built to work.

---

## The Red Thread (Why You Are Qualified)

You are not selling a theory. You are selling your own live journey:

1. You are building a 10K/10h freedom business right now
2. You use Human Design to decode HOW you work (not just what you do)
3. You use AI tools + Loom to sell without pressure
4. You teach from lived experience, not a script

**You are the case study.** Every client you take on follows the path you are walking.

---

## The Offer Stack (What They Get)

### Layer 1 — Human Design Decode
- Full chart reading focused on business application
- How they are built to sell, lead, create, and rest
- Their energetic "working hours" (not clock hours)

### Layer 2 — Decode Business Energy
- What to offer (based on their HD profile + expertise)
- When to push, when to pause
- How to stop the push-pressure cycle and finish cycles instead

### Layer 3 — How to Sell
- One AI-powered landing page for their core offer
- One Loom video walkthrough (their face, their voice, their proof)
- Simple DM-based sales process (no funnels required)

### Layer 4 — Move Despite Blocks
- Weekly accountability: what did you do, what stopped you?
- Practical session on each block as it shows up
- Live proof that imperfect action beats perfect planning

---

## The Container

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Length | 90 days |
| Format | Weekly 1:1 call (60 min) + async voice message support |
| Spots | 5 maximum |
| Delivery | Zoom + Loom + direct DM |
| Who it's for | Former personal trainers ready to move online |

---

## Pricing Architecture

| Tier | Price | Who |
|------|-------|-----|
| Standard | $3,500 | Public price |
| Founding client | $2,000 | First 3 clients from your existing list |

**Math check:** 3 founding clients × $2,000 = $6,000. 2 standard clients × $3,500 = $7,000.
Five clients = $13,000 — over $10K target. Done.

---

## The Positioning (What to Say)

**What you are NOT selling:**
- A course
- A generic coaching program
- Human Design readings as one-offs

**What you ARE selling:**
> "I'm building a 10K/10h freedom business right now, using my Human Design and AI tools.
> I have 5 spots for former personal trainers who want to do the same thing.
> We decode how you're built to work, package your expertise, and get your first sale in 90 days.
> I only take people I've talked to. DM me."

---

## How to Sell It (Your Natural Authority Method)

1. **Show the journey** — Loom video or post: "Here's what I'm building and how"
2. **Let them see themselves** — "This is for you if you're a PT who wants out of the hourly grind"
3. **Direct invitation** — DM to your existing list: "I have 5 spots, want to talk?"
4. **One conversation close** — No sales page needed to start. One call. One decision.

---

## What You Are Testing Right Now

| What you're doing | What it proves |
|-------------------|----------------|
| Building AI landing pages | You can teach this as part of the "how to sell" module |
| Making Loom videos | Your face + voice = the sales tool. Teach clients to do the same. |

**Every tool you build for yourself becomes a module for your clients.**

---

## The Anti-Pattern to Avoid

Do not position this as "Human Design coaching."

That's too abstract for personal trainers.

Position it as: **"10K in 10 hours — built around how you're wired to work."**
Human Design is the HOW behind the promise, not the headline.

---

## 72-Hour Starting Move

1. Write 3 names from your PT list
2. Send the DM above to all 3
3. Have a 20-minute call with whoever responds
4. Close one founding client at $2,000
5. Do the work. That's your first case study.
